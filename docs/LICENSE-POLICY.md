# Quantum Runtime License Policy

Quantum Runtime uses the Starlight Unit Studios Quantum Runtime Community Source License 1.0.

## Intended use

The license preserves free private and internal use while allowing Quantum Runtime to serve as an internal technical component of separate commercial applications and services.

A separate paid application may use Quantum Runtime behind its own product boundary when customers do not receive general-purpose access to Quantum Runtime administration, model administration, or an unrestricted Runtime API.

## Restricted use

Quantum Runtime itself may not be sold, rented, white-labeled, or offered as a paid standalone general-purpose Runtime or inference service without prior written permission.

## Distribution

A distributed modification must retain the required notices, identify itself as unofficial, provide corresponding source code, and remain under the same license.

## Third-party boundaries

Inference engines, models, model weights, datasets, tools, and other third-party components retain their own licenses. Quantum Runtime compatibility does not relicense those components.

The German text in `LICENSE.de.md` is legally controlling. This policy is explanatory and does not replace the license text.
