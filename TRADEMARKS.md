# Quantum Runtime Branding and Trademark Policy

## Protected project identity

The following names and visual elements identify the Starlight Unit Studios project:

- `Quantum Runtime`
- `Quantum Runtime by Starlight Unit Studios`
- `Starlight Unit Studios`
- associated official logos, word marks, interface marks, and origin notices

No trademark registration is asserted by this file unless separately documented. Copyright, naming, unfair-competition, and other applicable rights remain reserved.

## Required presentation

The product name is **Quantum Runtime**. A prominent first reference should preferably use `Quantum Runtime by Starlight Unit Studios`.

Built-in names, copyright notices, About information, source attribution, and official origin notices must remain visible and must not be removed, replaced, covered, or presented in a misleadingly minor role.

An operator may add a clearly subordinate notice such as `Operated by Example Organization`. The operator notice must not replace Quantum Runtime branding or imply that the operator is Starlight Unit Studios.

A publicly distributed modified version must state:

`This is an unofficial modification of Quantum Runtime and is not maintained or officially supported by Starlight Unit Studios.`

## Technical identifiers

Compatibility identifiers such as `quantum-runtime`, `QUANTUM_RUNTIME_*`, service names, package names, API paths, repository slugs, and domain components may remain unchanged. They are machine-facing identifiers and do not authorize another product to claim official Starlight Unit Studios status.

## Third-party names

Names such as Ollama, OpenAI, Gemma, Linux, and other third-party products are used only to describe compatibility or dependencies. Their marks and names remain subject to the rights of their respective owners.

## Permissions

Any branding change outside the terms of the Quantum Runtime Community Source License requires prior express written permission from the rights holder. Permission for one deployment does not create a general permission for another deployment.
